What Does a Month at Vaquero RV Park Actually Cost?

A month at Vaquero RV Park costs the rent on your lot and nothing else: electricity, water, sewer, trash and Wi-Fi are already in it. Right now the open lots run from $700 to $850 a month, with a $550 deposit you can pay in two parts.

Pleasanton sits on US Highway 281, about 37 miles south of downtown San Antonio. Shopping for a place to live usually means adding up a pile of separate bills: rent, electric, water, internet, a deposit and whatever shows up in the fine print. Here is the honest breakdown of what a month costs at Vaquero.

Right now there is a move-in offer on these four lots: $50 off the first month. That makes the first month $650 for Lot 17, $675 for Lot 10, $750 for Lot 21 and $800 for Lot 31. Regular rent starts in month two. The offer applies only to these four lots.

How does the $550 deposit work?

The deposit is $550 on any open lot, and you don't have to pay it all on day one. $300 is due at move-in, and the remaining $250 is due at the end of your first month. Moving is expensive enough without a lump sum landing the same week as the truck, the boxes and the first tank of gas.

What is already included in the rent?

Your rent covers {{UTILITIES}}, and the Wi-Fi is free and park-wide. That means no separate electric, water or internet bills to set up in your first week. The RVs above come with air conditioning already in place, and Lots 10, 17 and 21 are furnished, so there is no furniture to buy and nothing to assemble on your first night.

There is laundry on site and cameras around the park. Cats and small dogs are welcome, subject to approval. Ask us about your pet when you call, and we'll tell you straight whether it's a fit.

What does it cost to move in?

Take Lot 10 as an example. With the move-in offer, the first month's rent is $675, plus $300 of the deposit at move-in. Add those two together and that is what it takes to get the keys. The remaining $250 of the deposit is due at the end of that first month. From month two on, it's $725 a month with utilities and Wi-Fi included and nothing else to set up.

Compare that with a typical apartment move, where first month, a full deposit and separate deposits to start electric and internet can all come due before you have bought a single piece of furniture. At Vaquero you bring your clothes and your work gear, and you are home.

The same math works for the other lots. Swap in the first-month price above, add the $300, and you have your move-in number.

Do I have to sign a long lease?

No. Every lot at Vaquero is month-to-month. If the job runs longer than expected, you stay. If it ends, or you find a house, you're not locked into a lease you no longer need.
