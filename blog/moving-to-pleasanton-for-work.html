Moving to Pleasanton for Work? Here's How to Settle In Fast

A new job brings you to Atascosa County, and now you need a place to live fast. You don't have time to hunt for a house, buy furniture or sign a year-long lease before your first day. A park-owned RV on a month-to-month lease is built for exactly that.

Pleasanton sits on US Highway 281, about 37 miles south of downtown San Antonio (roughly {{DRIVE_MIN:Downtown_San_Antonio}} minutes by car). It is a small town with a slower pace, and close enough to the city for anything you can't find locally. Plenty of people who work in the area live here for a season of work, while they look for a house, or because they like the setup and just stay.

Can I move in without buying furniture?

Yes. At Vaquero RV Park you move into a park-owned RV that's ready on day one, with air conditioning already in place. Most of them come furnished, so you bring your clothes and your work gear, and you're home.

What does a month actually cost?

One of the hardest parts of moving for work is budgeting for a place you haven't lived in yet. Here the number is simple: your rent covers {{UTILITIES}}, including free park-wide Wi-Fi. There is laundry on site, parking, and cameras around the park.

That means no separate electric, water or internet bills to set up in your first week, and no surprise charges while you're still learning the town. What you see in the rent is what the month costs. See all our monthly RV lots in Pleasanton.

Can I bring my dog?

A move is stressful enough without leaving a pet behind. Cats and small dogs are welcome at Vaquero. Ask us about your pet when you call, and we'll tell you straight whether it's a fit.

What should I do in my first week?

  1. Call or text before you drive out. Lots turn over, and a quick message confirms what's open right now: {{PHONE}}.
  2. Come walk the lot. Photos help, but standing in the RV tells you in five minutes whether it fits your life.
  3. Ask what the month covers. At Vaquero it's rent, utilities and Wi-Fi, but always confirm the details for the lot you're looking at.
  4. Give yourself one easy evening. Your first night should be unpacking a bag, not assembling furniture.
