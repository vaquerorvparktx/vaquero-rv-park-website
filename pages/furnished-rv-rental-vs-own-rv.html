When renting our furnished RV makes sense

Rent ours if you need a place this month and don't own a rig. You skip buying an RV, finding a truck to tow it and paying to set it up. You bring your things and move in. It also fits a work assignment with no fixed end date: month-to-month rent, and nothing to sell when you go.

When bringing your own RV makes sense

Bring your own if you already have an RV or trailer. Your rent drops to {{RENT:lot30}} or {{RENT:lot50}} a month and the home stays yours when you move on. Most travel trailers run on 30-amp service. Larger rigs and fifth wheels with two air conditioners usually need 50 amp.

Questions

How much more does a furnished RV cost than a lot?

Our furnished RVs start at {{RENT:rvpark}} a month and a 30-amp lot is {{RENT:lot30}}, so the starting difference is what you pay for using our RV instead of owning one. Rent depends on the unit. Both include {{UTILITIES}}.

Do I need a truck or any RV experience to rent your RV?

No. Our RVs are already set up on their lots with power, water and sewer hooked up. You don't tow or level anything. Call or text {{PHONE}} for a showing and we'll walk you through the unit.

Can I start in your RV and bring my own later?

Ask us. It depends on which lots are open when you're ready. Call or text {{PHONE}} and tell us your plan, and we'll tell you what's coming available.

Are utilities included on a bare RV lot too?

Yes. Every rental at Vaquero RV Park includes {{UTILITIES}}, bare lots for your own rig too. A {{RENT:lot30}} lot is {{RENT:lot30}} a month, all in.
