The catch: you need an RV

A lot is just the space, with hookups. You need your own RV or travel trailer to live on it. If you don't have one, our furnished, park-owned RVs start at {{RENT:rvpark}} a month with the same {{UTILITIES}} included. Compare that number to an apartment's rent plus its bills.

How to compare in five minutes

  1. Get the apartment's monthly rent in writing.
  2. Ask which bills are yours: electricity, water, sewer, trash, internet.
  3. Ask the electric and water companies, or the last tenant, what those bills run in summer. South Texas summers push electric bills up.
  4. Add any monthly fees: pet rent, parking, amenity or admin fees.
  5. Compare the total with {{RENT:lot30}} or {{RENT:lot50}} for a lot, or from {{RENT:rvpark}} for one of our RVs.
