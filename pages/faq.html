Vaquero RV Park FAQ

Vaquero RV Park is a long-term, monthly RV park at 772 Humble Camp Rd in Pleasanton, Texas. RV lots start at {{RENT:lot30}} a month. If you don't own an RV, you can rent one of ours. These are the questions people ask us most, with straight answers.

Rent and costs

How much does it cost to live at Vaquero RV Park?

RV lots are {{RENT:lot30}} a month with 30-amp service or {{RENT:lot50}} a month with 50-amp service. Park-owned RVs start at {{RENT:rvpark}} a month and park-owned tiny homes start at {{RENT:tiny}} a month. Rent for park-owned homes depends on the unit. Each open home on our website lists its own rent and deposit.

Is there a deposit?

Yes. The deposit depends on the home, and each open home on our website lists its rent and deposit side by side, so you know the full move-in cost before you call. Call or text {{PHONE}} if you want to go over the numbers for a specific lot.

Are utilities included?

On the park-owned RVs open right now, yes. Their rent includes utilities, free Wi-Fi and laundry access. For a bare RV lot where you bring your own rig, call or text {{PHONE}} and we'll walk you through what the lot rent covers.

Is every park-owned RV the same price?

No. Park-owned RVs start at {{RENT:rvpark}} a month and the price depends on the unit. A two-bedroom costs more than a one-bedroom. The open homes on our website each show their exact rent, deposit and photos.

Stays and leases

Do you offer nightly or weekly stays?

No. Vaquero is a monthly, long-term RV park. Our leases are built for people who live here, so we don't take nightly, weekend or weekly stays. If you need a place for a month or longer in or around Pleasanton, we're a good fit.

Do I have to sign a long lease?

No. The homes open right now are rented month-to-month. You pay rent monthly through the resident portal and there's no long contract to lock you in. Call or text {{PHONE}} to talk about the lot you want and when you'd like to move in.

Homes and lots

What can I rent at Vaquero RV Park?

Five options: RV lots with 30-amp service ({{RENT:lot30}}/month), RV lots with 50-amp service ({{RENT:lot50}}/month), park-owned RVs (from {{RENT:rvpark}}/month), park-owned tiny homes (from {{RENT:tiny}}/month) and a few rent-to-own homes on select lots.

I don't own an RV. Can I still live here?

Yes. We rent park-owned RVs that are already set up on their lots, starting at {{RENT:rvpark}} a month. The ones open right now come furnished, with utilities, free Wi-Fi and laundry access included. You bring your things and move in.

Should I get a 30-amp or 50-amp lot?

Most travel trailers and smaller RVs run fine on 30-amp service, which is {{RENT:lot30}} a month. Larger rigs and fifth wheels with two air conditioners usually need 50-amp service, which is {{RENT:lot50}} a month. Check the plug on your RV, or call or text us and we'll help.

Do you have rent-to-own homes?

Yes, on select lots. Rent-to-own homes come open less often than our other rentals, so ask us what's coming up. Call or text {{PHONE}} and we'll tell you what's available and how it works.

Applying and moving in

How do I apply?

Send us the inquiry form on our website, or call or text {{PHONE}}. We'll follow up about the lot or home you want and set up a showing. Once you're approved, you pay rent and send maintenance requests through the resident portal.

Can I see the park before I move in?

Yes, and we recommend it. Call or text {{PHONE}} to set up a showing. We're at 772 Humble Camp Rd in Pleasanton, about {{DRIVE_MIN:H-E-B}} minutes from H-E-B, so it's an easy stop if you're in town.

Living here

Are pets allowed?

Yes. Cats and small dogs are welcome at Vaquero RV Park. A fenced off-leash dog park is coming with our Phase 2 expansion.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes. Free Wi-Fi covers the whole park through outdoor access points. It's set up for streaming, working from home and video calls, and it's included at no extra charge.

Is the park safe?

The park has 24-hour camera surveillance across the property. Controlled-entry security gates for residents and their guests are coming next.

Is there laundry?

Yes. The park-owned RVs open right now include laundry access in the rent. Call or text {{PHONE}} if you're bringing your own RV and want the details.

What's coming to the park?

Our Phase 2 expansion adds more lots, a fenced dog park and a kids play area. Controlled-entry security gates are also on the way.
